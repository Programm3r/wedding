Let’s make a weekend of it

Because one day just isn’t enough

We’d be honoured to have you spend the weekend with us as we celebrate our wedding. Rather than just a wedding day, we want a whole weekend with the people we love most. We’ve gathered our favourite people, found a beautiful spot in the Waterberg, and have the whole lodge to ourselves. Come relax, unwind, enjoy the bush, and share a few wonderful days together.

  1. Friday 13 August

    Arrive in the afternoon, settle in and just relax. As the sun goes down, we’ll gather around the fire for a pre-wedding celebration braai, with good company and laughter. The perfect start to a great weekend.

  2. Saturday 14 August

    The day we’ve been dreaming of, with the people we love most around us. We’ll say ‘I do’ and celebrate the beginning of our life together.

  3. Sunday 15 August

    Before everyone heads home, we’ll share one last breakfast together, swapping stories and laughing over the best moments of the night before.

The formal invitation, with all the wedding details, RSVPs and accommodation arrangements will follow closer to the time.

And no need to worry — the lodge has plenty of rooms for everyone.
