Our Story

Two hearts, one wild adventure

What started as a chance meeting became the greatest adventure of our lives. Through city lights and quiet weekends in the bush, we found home in each other — and now we can't wait to promise forever.

We would be honoured to have you with us beneath the African sky in the Waterberg mountains as we begin our next chapter together.

The Venue

Kuthaba Bush Lodge

A private game reserve tucked into the majestic Waterberg mountains — about 90 minutes from Pretoria. Dramatic cliffs, natural springs, over 200 bird species and 15 species of game set the scene for our day.

Getting there

From Johannesburg or Pretoria, take the N1 north towards Polokwane and exit at Modimolle (Nylstroom). The lodge is on Driefontein Road in the Waterberg. Some gravel roads are involved — a normal sedan is fine, just take it slow.

Please plan to arrive before sunset: game roads are much easier to navigate in daylight.

Good to Know

Questions & Answers

What will the weather be like?

Mid-August is winter in the Waterberg: clear, sunny days around 20–24 °C, but nights can drop close to freezing. The reception is open-air around a bonfire, so pack warm layers for the evening.

When should I arrive and leave?

Check-in opens at 2:00 pm on Friday 13 August, and we'd love everyone there for the welcome braai that evening. Check-out is by 10:00 am on Sunday 15 August, after breakfast. If Friday is impossible, come through on Saturday morning instead — just tell us so we can plan.

Can I bring a plus-one or children?

Your invitation will say who is included. If you're unsure, please get in touch with us before you RSVP.

What shoes should I wear?

The ceremony and reception are on natural ground and river sand. Block heels, wedges or smart flats are your friends — leave the stilettos at home.

Will there be wildlife around?

The lodge sits inside a private game reserve with plains game and over 200 bird species. Keep an eye out on the drive in, and please follow lodge guidance after dark.

Do you have a gift registry?

Your presence is the greatest gift. If you'd like to spoil us, details will follow with the formal invitation.
